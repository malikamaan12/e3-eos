# Controlled documents, evidence and tender submission

## Connected workspaces

The Company Evidence Vault holds reusable masters such as CR, trade licences, financial statements, CVs, project references, completion certificates, HSE/QA policies, insurance, letterhead, approved logos and templates. A Project Document Workspace tracks tender requirements, working files, reviews and project derivatives. A Submission Pack Builder pins selected revisions, order, export settings, approval and the exact issued output.

Reuse a shared document identity, file revision, approval, comments and audit layer. Selecting a vault revision for a project must not mutate the company master. A referenced external file needs an immutable captured version when used in an approval or submitted pack.

## Document contract

Keep stable document/master ID, revision ID, original immutable bytes/checksum, file role, issuer, entity, owner, category, reporting period, validity state, language, verification, permitted use, sensitivity and source. Distinguish original from editable source, preview, issued PDF, certified copy, translation and redacted derivative. Latest upload is not automatically approved or current.

An RFP document register can include a required item even while its file is missing. Its status tracks missing, draft, under review, approved for intended use, expired/unsuitable, waived by authority, or issued. Missing evidence must remain visible through the tender gate.

## Submission pack

The pack can produce one combined PDF or the separately ordered files/envelopes demanded by a tender. Before finalization, preview sequence, page orientation, filename, signatures/stamps, redactions, expiry and required-document coverage. Apply a signature/stamp asset only with controlled authority and record who applied it and to which revision/pages. Finalization records an immutable output checksum and a manifest of every selected source revision and configuration. A correction makes a new pack revision; never rewrite what was sent.

Client sharing is explicit and scoped to approved files. Internal costs, supplier rates, margins, bank information and unpublished draft comments are excluded from client projections. Retain transmission/receipt evidence without claiming submission merely from a generated PDF.
